Asking before running

Most calls to Pulse never read a record. This film follows an agent through the calls it makes before it runs anything: the session-bootstrap order, the manifest and its intent scope, feature profiles, inspect, question kinds and their guides, recommend, predict and its advisories, strict input, error fixups, explain, request params and templates, and a facet as a cheap first look at the data.

0:00

    The cohort is illustrative: 800 generated shoppers in an A/B test. Every manifest entry, schema, draft, verdict, error, fixup, advisory, count, mean and p-value on a result card comes from a recorded Pulse v1.0.0-alpha.33 run in this film's data folder, including one MCP session under a feature profile. The template is shown as a file; it is not rendered there, because the CLI has no template command. Tool names, codes and slot names follow the Pulse source and skill pack. Pulse adds operators, skills and examples over time; your instance's manifest lists what it offers.
