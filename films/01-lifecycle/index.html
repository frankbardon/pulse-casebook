Pulse Anatomy · film 1 · narrated

The lifecycle of a request

One request follows its path from bytes on disk to a JSON envelope. It is decoded and admitted, checked against the schema, routed to an execution arm and streamed through the record loop across four shards. Then the response is folded, shaped and wrapped, and along the way we see what Pulse refuses, and how one failed slot fails alone.
